<Chapter eyebrow="The problem" title="Up to six weeks to find one insight.">
  <Media slot="media" src="sorcero/article-card.webp" alt="Literature monitoring article card with highlighted drug mentions and knowledge graph" tone="sor" ratio="16/9" large />

Medical Affairs teams sift clinical trial data, publications and health records by hand, across spreadsheets and slide decks. One insight could take six weeks.

<Expander summary="What the wait costs">

Six weeks is the average just to work through the spreadsheets, presentations and publications. Spotting a trend and briefing internal and external stakeholders takes longer still. In life sciences, time spent waiting for answers costs millions in lost opportunities, money and lives.

</Expander>
</Chapter>

<Chapter eyebrow="Iterations" title="Our first direction missed.">
  <MediaGrid slot="media" min={260}>
    <Media tone="sor" tag="Iteration · vendor-led dashboard" ratio="4/3" />
    <Media tone="sor2" tag="Iteration · action items and stages" ratio="4/3" />
  </MediaGrid>

We started with a third-party vendor and a clear plan, but had not validated it with customers. They found it too complicated: action items and stages added steps, and nothing helped them group insights from the field.

<Expander summary="What customers asked for instead">

Action items, stages and navigating from the dashboard added steps to a workflow that was already long. What customers wanted was simpler:

1. Upload insights.
2. Have them organized and tagged.
3. Group them.
4. See the data visually.

</Expander>
</Chapter>
