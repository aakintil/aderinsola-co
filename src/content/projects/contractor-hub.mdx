<Chapter eyebrow="01 · The problem" title="Leads arrived. Nobody answered.">

Homeowners were reaching out, but contractors had no place to see, sort or respond to them. Only 18% of leads got a contractor response, and there was no single place to join the programs that paid for the work.

</Chapter>

<Chapter eyebrow="02 · Lead management" title="An inbox built for a busy installer.">
  <MediaGrid slot="media" min={300}>
    <Media src="hub/leads.webp" alt="Lead management list composition" tone="hub" ratio="1" size="half" />
    <Media src="hub/lead-detail.webp" alt="Lead detail composition" tone="sio" ratio="1" size="half" />
  </MediaGrid>

Every lead in one list, with the job, location and status up front, and a single action to acknowledge it. In the same window, leads that got the fastest response went from 1% to 32%.

</Chapter>

<Chapter eyebrow="03 · Onboarding" title="Licenses, business, service areas. Done.">
  <Media slot="media" src="hub/onboarding.webp" alt="Contractor onboarding composition" tone="nike" ratio="16/10" large />

A short, guided application. Colorado contractors finish it more often than SIO contractors do. The licenses step is still the biggest drop-off, with about 44% leaving before basic info, and it is the next thing I would fix.
</Chapter>

<Chapter eyebrow="05 · Built to travel" title="The next state starts from here.">

</Chapter>
