<Chapter eyebrow="01 · The problem" title="Leads arrived. Nobody answered.">

Homeowners were reaching out, but contractors had no place to see, sort or respond to them. Only 18% of leads got a contractor response, and there was no single place to join the programs that paid for the work.

</Chapter>

<Chapter eyebrow="02 · Lead management" title="An inbox built for a busy installer.">
  <MediaGrid slot="media" cols={2} stack>
    <Media src="hub/leads.webp" alt="Leads page: one new lead this week, a 90-day breakdown of 3 accepted, 5 not responded and 2 declined, a Fast response rate, and lead cards with Decline and Accept buttons" bg="var(--t-paper)" size="half" />
    <Media src="hub/lead-detail.webp" alt="A lead marked Action needed: job type Heat pumps and HVAC, Decline and Accept buttons, the inquiry date, an Apply with Eli rebate link, customer details and a map" bg="var(--t-paper)" size="half" />

Every lead in one list, with the job, contact and inquiry date up front, and one choice to make: accept or decline. In the same window, leads that got the fastest response went from 1% to 32%. The screens on this page are from the California setup; Colorado's are the same product with its own logo, colors and programs.

</Chapter>

<Chapter eyebrow="03 · Onboarding" title="Licenses, business, service areas. Done.">
  <Media slot="media" src="hub/onboarding.webp" alt="Edit business license: upload a renewed license to have its details read for you, then issuing state, license number and expiry date, with the license types the program accepts" bg="var(--t-paper)" large />

A short, guided application. Colorado contractors finish it more often than SIO contractors do. The licenses step is still the biggest drop-off, with about 44% leaving before basic info, and it's the next thing I'd fix.
</Chapter>

Colorado runs the same product as California, with its own logo, programs and copy, set up as configuration rather than a rebuild. The Colorado Hub helped BDC win a competitively bid contract of up to $4.5M from the Denver Regional Council of Governments. The same foundation runs the companion homeowner site, which sent 689 leads to contractors in its first ten months of tracking.

</Chapter>
