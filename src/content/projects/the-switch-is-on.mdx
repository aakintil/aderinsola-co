<Chapter eyebrow="01 · The problem" title="Great tools nobody could find.">
  <Media slot="media" tone="sio" tag="Old homepage, 2024, above the fold" ratio="16/9" large />

In 2024, only about 1 in 70 visitors ever reached the Incentive Finder. And the site looked so much like a paid lead marketplace that visitors kept asking what we were selling.

<Expander summary="What we saw">

Rebates were the top reason people visited, but the Incentive Finder sat below the fold behind dense copy and stock imagery. Contractors landed on a homeowner site and assumed it was not for them, so they never found the free leads. And when the site explained that it was a nonprofit, user testing showed it sounded defensive and made people more suspicious.

</Expander>
</Chapter>

What do you need? Two paths above the fold, find incentives or find a pro, with each module below doing one job and measured on its own.

<Expander summary="The modules">

1. Hero: clear in three seconds
2. Tool crossroads: route to incentives or contractors
3. Benefit carousel: the four "better" themes
4. About SIO: trust shown through behavior, not claims
5. Contractor gateway: free leads for pros

<Chapter eyebrow="05 · The design system" title="The system had stalled twice. I changed how it got made.">
  <MediaGrid slot="media" min={220}>
    <Media src="sio/ds-color.webp" alt="Design system color palettes" tone="sio" ratio="4/5" size="third" />
    <Media src="sio/ds-buttons.webp" alt="Design system buttons" tone="hub" ratio="4/5" size="third" />
    <Media src="sio/ds-cards.webp" alt="Design system cards" tone="cigna" ratio="4/5" size="third" />
  </MediaGrid>

Past attempts died in review, not in build. I became the single decision owner, moved pages over one at a time as they were touched, and made a shared checklist the definition of done.

<Expander summary="When the plan changed">

I built a token pipeline and a custom Figma plugin to keep design and code in sync. Engineering then decided against a layer between Figma and code. Rather than defend the work, I collapsed the system to 13 role colors defined the same way for every brand, made the code the source of truth, and kept the plugin as a backup.

</Expander>
</Chapter>

<Chapter eyebrow="06 · The finders" title="Trust is won on the card, not the profile.">
  <Media slot="media" src="sio/confirmed-installs.webp" alt="Contractor cards with verified licenses and confirmed installs" fit="cover" large />

Three finders had grown their own shells, filters and cards. The data showed homeowners decide on the card, so that is where I put the proof: verified licenses and confirmed installs. The rule for merging them: conform first, then converge.

<Expander summary="Partner badges">

Every funding program wanted its badge shown, and prominently. Prioritizing paid partners would have quietly pushed down unpaid programs. We landed on fixed-size badges in their own row above the contact button, and accepted two columns instead of three so badges never wrap.

</Expander>

<Chapter eyebrow="07 · Reach" title="One system, many brands.">
  <Media slot="media" src="sio/field-2.webp" alt="An installer working on a heat pump unit in a crawl space" fit="cover" ratio="16/8" large />
