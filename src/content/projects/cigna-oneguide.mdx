<Chapter eyebrow="The problem" title="Two apps, one confused customer.">

Guidance lived in Compass. Coverage lived in myCigna. Customers had to stitch the two together, so most of them picked up the phone.

<Expander summary="What we set out to do">

1. Reduce the number of customer phone calls
2. Give quick access to personal health information
3. Make it easier to find care, lower-cost options, and pre and post-surgery support
4. Turn moments of doubt into a calm digital experience

</Expander>
</Chapter>
